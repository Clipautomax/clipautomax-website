Privacy Policy

How ClipAutoMax collects, uses, and protects visitor and client data across our platform.

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When storefront visitors interact with the ClipAutoMax AI Concierge, we collect information submitted during chat conversations (such as email addresses, sizing preferences, or product questions). We also collect standard usage analytics to ensure system uptime.

2. How We Use Information

Visitor chat data is used solely to answer product queries and route lead contact details to your designated Google Sheets account. We do not sell, rent, or trade visitor or client data to third parties under any circumstances.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

Our concierge widget and automation workflows utilize secure third-party infrastructure providers (including Voiceglow / ConvoCore, Make.com, and Google Workspace APIs). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Store owners can request the complete deletion of their catalog training data and logged leads at any time by contacting our support team at clipautomax@gmail.com.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email clipautomax@gmail.com.
