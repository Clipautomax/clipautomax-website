Privacy Policy

How Clipautomax handles, respects, and protects data collected through our website and AI concierge widget.

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. Data Collection & Lead Flow

When visitors interact with our AI concierge widget (ConvoCore), we may collect name, email, phone number, and chat message content voluntarily provided in chat. Leads flow via Make.com automation and are logged into a private Google Sheet accessible only to Clipautomax and the client store owner.

2. No Data Selling Guarantee

We strictly do NOT sell, rent, or trade visitor data to third parties. Data is shared only with processors necessary to deliver the service (ConvoCore, Make.com, Google Workspace).

3. Data Retention Policy

Client lead records and chatbot interaction summaries are retained for the duration of the active subscription. Upon subscription cancellation or written termination, lead data stored within managed Google Sheets and automation logs is permanently deleted within 30 days, unless longer retention is required by law.

4. User Rights & Contact

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing clipautomax@gmail.com.
